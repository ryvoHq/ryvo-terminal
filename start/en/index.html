Workspaces

One tab per task.

Start, Scalper, Swing, Options, Derivatives, On-chain, Macro, News: each tab shows only the tiles that belong to that job. A command line opens any market and any function.

  • Command line and search across all functions
  • Save your own workspaces and share them as a link
  • Drag and resize tiles, pop them out into their own window

Order flow

Who buys, who sells – at each price level.

Footprint, delta and CVD show which side traded. The price ladder and the tape make large orders and speed visible.

  • Footprint with imbalance and delta
  • CVD for spot and futures side by side
  • Volume profile and market profile

Derivatives

Funding, open interest, liquidations.

See funding per exchange, the change in open interest and real liquidations on the tape. The liquidation map is a model – and labelled as one.

  • Funding annualised, with countdown
  • Liquidation map with its model explained

Options and macro

Full option chain, volatility surface, max pain and probabilities at expiry. Plus the US yield curve, the euro area, the economic calendar and futures positioning from official sources.

  • Option chain with Greeks, volatility and open interest

  • Stocks, indices, gold and oil chartable as contracts

Statistics with a cross-check

We show the cross-check.

Many trading rules sound convincing. We measure them against the data – and put every measurement next to chance. If a zone holds no more often than a randomly shifted one, that is exactly what it says.

Zone test bench

Prior-day high, pivots, σ levels: how often did the zone hold? Next to it, the same measurement for 200 runs of randomly shifted zones.

High/low clock

In which hour were the daily high and low made? Compared with a pure random walk.

σ range

How many days closed within ±1 and ±2 σ? Compared with the normal distribution, with sample size and period.

Every estimate names its model. Past frequencies are not a forecast.

Overlay and indicators

The numbers where you are already looking.

RYVO Overlay is a browser extension: a small panel with price, funding, open interest, liquidation levels and large trades on top of the chart of your exchange or charting site. Level indicators for charting software come with it.

  • Floats above the chart, can be moved and minimised
  • Fetches public market data only – no keys, no orders
  • Notes state distances to levels, never an action

Status: built, not yet published. There is no download and no store listing here.

Questions and answers

Does it cost anything?

No. The beta is free, with no account and no installation. A Pro version with additional tools is planned; price and date are not fixed.

Is this investment advice? Are there signals?

No. The terminal shows data and calculates metrics, the same for everyone. It never says “buy” or “sell” and does not know your personal situation.

Where does the data come from?

Your browser loads it directly from exchanges and public bodies, for example Bybit, Binance, Hyperliquid, the CFTC, the US Treasury, the ECB and Eurostat. We run no data server of our own. The full list is under Licences and Privacy.

Can I trade real money?

No. Trading is play money only. No order goes to any exchange, we accept no money and hold nothing.

Why is an exchange missing for me?

Some exchanges cannot be reached from certain countries. The terminal then uses another source and shows which one at the top. If everything fails, it says “no data” instead of showing old values.

Which devices does it run on?

In current browsers on desktop and phone; on a phone it can be added to the home screen like an app. Languages: German, English, Spanish, French, Italian, Turkish.

What do you store about me?

Nothing on a server. Settings and the practice account live only in your browser. There are no cookies, no analytics and no ads.

One click and it runs.

No account, no installation. You see real market data straight away.
